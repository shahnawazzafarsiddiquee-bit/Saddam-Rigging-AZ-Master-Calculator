# Privacy Policy

**App:** Rigging & Lifting Calculator (Saddam Rigging A-Z Master Calculator)
**Package:** `com.saddam.rigging.azmaster`
**Last updated:** 21 September 2026

## Summary

This app is an offline engineering tool for rigging, lifting and crane
planning. It does not have user accounts, it does not show ads, and it does
not send your data anywhere. Everything you enter stays on your own device.

## Information we collect

**We collect nothing.** The app has no servers, no analytics, no tracking and
no advertising libraries. We cannot see anything you type into it.

Everything you create — equipment records, lift plans, inspections, JSAs,
risk assessments, toolbox talks, calculation results and backups — is stored
in a private database on your device and never leaves it unless you choose to
share or export it yourself.

## Permissions and why they are needed

| Permission | Why the app asks for it |
|---|---|
| **Camera** | To scan equipment QR codes and to read text from load charts and plates using on-device text recognition. Images are processed on your device and are never uploaded or stored by us. |
| **Photos / storage** | To save PDF reports and backup files, and to let you pick an existing image for text recognition. Only on Android 12 and older; newer versions use the system file picker instead. |
| **Internet** | Used only so Google Play services can supply the offline text-recognition component, and for the Android share and print features you trigger yourself. No personal data is sent to us, because we operate no servers. |

## Sharing your information

We never share your information, because we never receive it. Data leaves
your device only when **you** deliberately act — for example exporting a lift
plan as a PDF, sharing a report through another app, printing, or saving a
backup file. Where that data goes is then governed by the app or service you
chose.

## Data retention and deletion

Your data stays on your device until you delete it. You can remove everything
at any time by deleting individual records in the app, or by uninstalling the
app, which erases its entire local database. Backup files you exported
yourself must be deleted by you from wherever you saved them.

## Children

This app is a professional tool for industrial and construction work. It is
not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect any information from
anyone.

## Security

Because all data stays in the app's private storage on your device, its
security depends on your device's own protection. We recommend keeping a
screen lock enabled and keeping Android up to date.
