# Privacy Policy – Saddam Rigging A-Z Master Calculator

Last updated: 24 September 2026

Saddam Rigging A-Z Master Calculator ("the app") is a rigging, lifting and crane-planning calculator. This policy explains what information the app handles.

## Summary

- The app does **not** collect, sell or share any personal data.
- There is **no account, no login, no ads and no analytics**.
- Everything you enter stays **on your own phone**.

## Data stored on your device

The app saves the following only in a local database on your device, so that you can use it again later:

- Lift plans, job logbook entries and calculation inputs
- Equipment records, certificate expiry dates and inspection checklists
- Photos you choose to attach to inspections
- Favourites and app settings

This data never leaves your device unless you choose to share it (see below). Uninstalling the app deletes it.

## Camera and photos

The camera is used only when you choose to:

- scan an equipment QR code,
- take a photo for an inspection, or
- read text from a crane load chart photo (OCR).

OCR runs on your device using Google ML Kit on-device text recognition. Images are not uploaded by the app.

## Sharing and backups

When you tap Share, Print or Backup, the app creates a PDF or backup file and hands it to the Android share or print screen. You decide where it goes (for example WhatsApp, email or Google Drive). The app does not send it anywhere by itself.

## Children

The app is a professional tool for riggers, lifting supervisors and engineers. It is not directed at children under 13.
